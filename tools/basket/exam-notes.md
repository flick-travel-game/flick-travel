# Basketball exam — fact-check notes (checked 2026-10-10)

## Rule versions (main finding)
- **Current FIBA rules = FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026**, approved by the FIBA Central Board in Berlin on 23 April 2026, valid as of **1 October 2026** (previous: OBR 2024, valid from 1 Oct 2024). Verified from the rulebook PDF text itself.
  - https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-fiba-official-rules-2026-v1-1.pdf
  - https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-fiba-official-rules-2026-rule-changes-v1-1-en.pdf
  - https://about.fiba.basketball/en/news/fiba-official-basketball-rules-2026-to-take-effect-october-1
- **Surprise:** the *unsportsmanlike foul no longer exists* in OBR 2026. It is split into **disruptive foul** (Art. 37, does not count toward disqualification) and **flagrant foul** (Art. 38, counts). Technical fouls split into **Category 1** (counts toward GD) and **Category 2** (does not). So any question saying "FIBA = unsportsmanlike vs NBA = flagrant" is now outdated; both now use "flagrant".
- **JBA (Japan):** uses it as the "2027 バスケットボール競技規則". B.LEAGUE / W League applied it early from the 2026-27 opening (B.LEAGUE opened 22 Sep 2026); Emperor's/Empress's Cup final round from Jan 2027; other domestic competitions from 1 Apr 2027. Until then other competitions use the "2026年版" (based on FIBA 2024, in force 1 Apr 2026). The JBA summary PDF (2027rule_changepoint_20260823.pdf) now returns 404; the details come from the search-engine extract of it plus the B.LEAGUE notice.
  - https://www.bleague.jp/news_detail/id=621140
  - https://basket-count.com/article/detail/274448
  - https://japanbasketball.jp/files/referee/rule/2026rule.pdf (2026年版, FIBA 2024 base)
- **NBA:** official.nba.com links the **2026-27 Official NBA Playing Rules** as current. https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/10/2026-27-Official-NBA-Playing-Rules.pdf . No on-court rule changes for 2026-27 found in news (2025-26 change: Replay Center decides proximate foul on out-of-bounds challenges — https://pr.nba.com/nba-cup-semifinal-host-sites-coachs-challenge-rules).
- **3x3:** FIBA 3x3 Rules 2026 valid as of 1 Jan 2026 (Korean federation copy of the official rulebook): court 15 m x 11 m, 10 min or 21 pts, OT first to 2 pts, 12 s shot clock, 1/2 pts, team fouls 7-9 = 2 FT, 10+ = 2 FT + possession. https://www.koreabasketball.or.kr/static/2026_FIBA_3x3.pdf ; JBA domestic from 1 Apr 2026: https://www.japanbasketball.jp/files/referee/rule/fiba-3x3-basketballrules_revisions_20251204.pdf

## Ambiguous / corrected items
- **No-charge semi-circle:** the brief said 1.25 m. The FIBA 2026 text says radius **1.30 m measured to the outer edge** (the 1.25 m figure is to the inner edge with a 5 cm line). I wrote 1.30 m (to outer edge). NBA restricted area = 4 ft half-circle (verified in NBA rulebook).
- **Team-foul penalty:** FIBA = penalty after 4 team fouls in a quarter (i.e., from the 5th). NBA = "four team fouls per regulation period without additional penalties"; if fewer than 4 by the last 2 min, one more foul allowed without penalty (so 2nd foul in last 2 min). Overtime: NBA penalty after 3; FIBA OT fouls count as 4th quarter.
- **Head coach's challenge (FIBA):** OBR 2026 Appendix F.4 — one HCC per game, only where IRS is used. The year HCC was first introduced was **not verified** (brief guessed 2024; 2024 changes only added the ability to withdraw a challenge). NBA: 1 challenge, a 2nd if the first succeeds.
- **Goaltending difference:** FIBA allows touching the ball after it touches the ring (except touching basket/backboard while ball on ring, etc.); NBA forbids touching the ball in the cylinder above the ring. Did not find evidence FIBA "aligned" with NBA.
- **Gather step / "zero step":** NBA rulebook defines "the gather"; "zero step" is a popular term, not in either rulebook text. FIBA allows two steps after gaining control (Art. 25).
- **Naismith:** first game date Dec 21, 1891 (Britannica, Hall of Fame 1891 gallery), but Britannica Kids gives 20 Jan 1892 for "first organized/public game". Rule count "13" is standard; one source says 10. The "18 students" figure was not verified — omitted.
  - https://springfield.edu/about/birthplace-of-basketball ; https://www.hoophall.com/explore/exhibits/1891 ; https://www.britannica.com/sports/history-of-basketball
- **Japan men Paris 2024:** "first qualification by own merit since Montreal 1976 (48 years)" — sources say "first outright qualification since 1976"; one Olympics.com article oddly says "1978". "48 years" is my arithmetic (1976→2024).
  - https://www.fiba.basketball/en/news/basketballworldcup-2023-news-japan-clinch-berth-to-paris-2024-as-best-placed-team-from-asia ; https://olympics.com/en/news/paris-2024-men-s-basketball-team-preview-japan
- **Yudai Baba:** never played an NBA regular-season game (Mavericks preseason 2019, then G League Texas Legends). Japanese players with NBA regular-season games: Tabuse, Watanabe, Hachimura, Kawamura. Wrote that explicitly.
- **Eurostep:** Ginóbili popularized it; Šarūnas Marčiulionis is often credited with bringing it to the NBA (ESPN). Brief mentioned Jasikevičius — not supported.
  - https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/25206602/most-dangerous-two-step-game
- **Tim Hardaway:** popularized but did not invent the crossover ("UTEP two-step" → "killer crossover"). https://www.si.com/nba/2022/09/08/manu-ginobili-tim-hardaway-crossover-eurostep-signature-moves-hall-of-fame
- **Wembanyama 2024 ROY unanimous** — not verified, omitted.
- **2027 men's World Cup (Qatar, 27 Aug–12 Sep 2027):** AP (June 2026) reported FIBA still planning for Qatar but could adjust because of the Iran conflict. Treat as "予定". https://wtop.com/news/2026/06/fiba-still-planning-for-basketball-world-cup-in-qatar-in-2027-as-scheduled/
- **Women's World Cup 2026:** USA 97-79 France, 13 Sep 2026, Berlin; 12th title, 5th straight; MVP Breanna Stewart. https://www.nba.com/news/u-s-women-win-fifth-straight-womens-fiba-world-cup-title . 2030 host Japan (Tokyo, 26 Nov–8 Dec 2030): https://about.fiba.basketball/en/news/fiba-selects-japan-as-2030-womens-world-cup-host-france-as-host-of-2031-mens-world-cup

- **JBA founding name:** sources vary between 大日本籠球協会 / 大日本バスケットボール協会 (1930). I wrote 大日本籠球協会.
